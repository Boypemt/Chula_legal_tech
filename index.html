What's included

  • No build step

    Open index.html or serve the folder. No bundler, no install, nothing to configure.

  • Design tokens

    Colours, spacing, and typography live as CSS custom properties in one place, with light and dark themes.

  • Accessible shell

    Skip link, labelled controls, keyboard-friendly navigation, and visible focus styles out of the box.

  • Room to grow

    Plain ES modules in js/, so you can add a framework or a bundler later without undoing anything.
